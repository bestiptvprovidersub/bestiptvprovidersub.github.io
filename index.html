Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What equipment do I need to stream IPTV?

All you need is a stable internet connection (25+ Mbps recommended for seamless 4K streaming) and a compatible device such as an Amazon Firestick, Smart TV, Apple TV, Formuler box, Android box, tablet, or smartphone.

Can I stream on multiple devices simultaneously?

Standard plans cover 1 active connection at a time. If you want Multiroom viewing across several screens in your home, multi-connection options are readily available at discounted rates.

What is the difference between M3U and Xtream Codes API?

Both grant access to the exact same channel library. However, Xtream Codes API (Username, Password, and Server URL) is faster to configure and automatically organizes EPG TV guides and channel icons.
